Privacy Policy

Last updated October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MSP Sprout (“MSP Sprout,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and shares personal information when you visit mspsprout.com (the “Site”), send us a message, or book a call with us.

By using the Site, you agree to the practices described here. If you don’t agree, please don’t use the Site.

Information We Collect

Information you give us

  • Contact form: your name, email address, company name, phone number, and any message you write.
  • Booking a call: when you click a booking button, you’re taken to a scheduling page run by Cal.com. Any details you enter there, such as your name, email, and answers to booking questions, are collected through that service.
  • Email and other communication: anything you send us directly.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and browser details, such as browser type, operating system, and screen size.
  • Your IP address and approximate location.
  • Pages you visit, links you click, how long you stay, and the page that referred you.

This information is collected through server logs, cookies, and similar technologies, described below.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your messages and schedule calls.
  • To share information about our services when you ask for it.
  • To understand how the Site is used and improve it.
  • To measure whether our advertising is working.
  • To keep the Site secure and prevent spam and abuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Cookies, Analytics, and Advertising Technologies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We and our service providers may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences, understand traffic, and measure advertising performance.

We may use tools such as Google Analytics and Google Ads conversion tracking. These tools can collect information about your visit and use it to report on traffic and ad results. They may also be used to show you our ads on other sites.

You can control this in several ways:

  • Change your browser settings to block or delete cookies. Some parts of the Site may not work as well if you do.
  • Manage Google ad personalization at adssettings.google.com.
  • Install Google’s Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Third-Party Services

We rely on other companies to run the Site. They process information under their own privacy policies:

  • Netlify: hosts the Site and receives contact form submissions.
  • Cal.com: runs our call scheduling.
  • Google Fonts: the Site loads fonts from Google, which may receive your IP address and browser details when it does.
  • YouTube: some blog posts embed YouTube videos. YouTube may set cookies and collect data when you play a video.
  • Google Analytics and Google Ads: used for analytics and ad measurement, as described above.

The Site also links to other websites, such as LinkedIn and YouTube. We’re not responsible for how those sites handle your information.

How We Share Information

We don’t sell your personal information.

We share it only with the service providers listed above and others who help us operate the Site and our business, when required by law or to protect rights and safety, and in connection with a merger, sale, or similar business transaction.

How Long We Keep Information

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes in this policy, including to follow up on your inquiry, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes. After that, we delete or de-identify it.

Your Choices and Rights

You can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. If you receive marketing emails from us, you can opt out at any time with the unsubscribe link or by contacting us.

Depending on where you live, such as California, the European Economic Area, or the United Kingdom, you may have additional rights under local law. To make a request, contact us using the details below, and we’ll respond within the time the law requires.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect personal information. No website or method of transmission is completely secure, so we can’t guarantee absolute security.

Children’s Privacy

The Site is for businesses and isn’t directed to children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have, contact us and we’ll delete it.

International Visitors

The Site and our service providers may process information in countries other than where you live, including the United States. By using the Site, you understand your information may be transferred there.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we’ll change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.

Contact Us

Questions or requests about this policy? Reach us through our contact page.
