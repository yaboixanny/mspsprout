Fractional CMO Questions, Answered

The questions we hear most from MSPs before starting a Fractional CMO engagement with us.

What does a Fractional CMO actually do for an MSP?
A Fractional CMO sets your overall marketing strategy, owns your budget allocation across channels, manages the vendors and team actually executing the work, and reports on pipeline and revenue instead of vanity marketing metrics, all without sitting on your payroll full-time.
How is this different from just hiring an agency for one channel?
A single-channel agency optimizes whatever channel they were hired to run, whether or not that channel is actually the right one for your growth goals. A Fractional CMO sits above every channel, decides where your budget should go, and holds every vendor, including us, accountable to the same pipeline numbers.
Do you replace our internal marketing person, or work alongside them?
Either. Some clients have us lead a small internal team or freelancers; others have no marketing hire yet and we build the function from scratch. We fit into whatever setup you already have.
How much does a Fractional CMO cost compared to a full-time hire?
A full-time CMO typically runs well into six figures in salary alone, plus benefits and ramp-up time. A Fractional CMO engagement gives you the same strategic oversight at a fraction of that cost, scaled to what your MSP actually needs right now.
Do you require a long-term contract?
No multi-year lock-in. We ask for enough runway to build and start executing a real strategy, but you can adjust or pause the engagement as your needs change.
