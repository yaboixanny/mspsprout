Cybersecurity & MSSP Marketing Questions, Answered

The questions we hear most from cybersecurity companies and MSSPs before starting a program with us.

How is marketing for a cybersecurity company or MSSP different from marketing for a typical MSP?
Security buyers search and evaluate differently. They’re often researching compliance requirements (SOC 2, HIPAA, PCI-DSS), comparing SOC and MDR capabilities, and checking certifications before they ever pick up the phone. Our keyword research, ad targeting, and page content are built around that buying process, not a generic IT-services template.
Do you target compliance-driven keywords like SOC 2, HIPAA, or PCI-DSS?
Yes. Compliance-driven searches are some of the highest-intent traffic for cybersecurity companies and MSSPs, so we build dedicated content and landing pages around the specific frameworks and certifications your ideal clients are already searching for.
Can you run Google Ads for us if we already have SEO in place, or vice versa?
Yes. Most clients run SEO and Google Ads together, since SEO builds long-term organic visibility while ads produce immediate leads for high-intent searches. We can start with either channel and layer in the other once the foundation is in place.
Do you handle outbound lead generation too, or just inbound marketing?
Both. Alongside SEO and paid ads, we run outbound campaigns that target IT directors, CISOs, and compliance officers directly, so you’re not only relying on inbound search traffic to fill the pipeline.
Do you require a long-term contract?
We recommend at least a few months to let campaigns and rankings start moving, but there’s no multi-year lock-in—you can adjust or pause the program as your needs change.
