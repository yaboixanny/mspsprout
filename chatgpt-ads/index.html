ChatGPT Ads Questions, Answered

The questions we hear most from MSPs curious about advertising inside ChatGPT.

Are ChatGPT ads actually available right now?
OpenAI has been piloting ad and sponsored-placement formats inside ChatGPT, and availability is expanding. We track every rollout closely and apply for pilot access on behalf of clients so your MSP is positioned to move the moment a placement becomes available in your category.
What if ChatGPT ads aren’t available in my category yet?
We still build the GEO (generative engine optimization) foundation that earns you organic citations in ChatGPT’s answers today, so you’re not waiting on paid placement to start showing up—and you’ll be first in line once ad placements open up.
How is this different from your AI SEO / GEO service?
AI SEO/GEO focuses on organic citations across ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity. This service adds a ChatGPT-specific layer on top—tracking and applying for paid placement opportunities as OpenAI rolls them out, specifically inside ChatGPT.
Is this a long-term contract?
No. Given how early and fast-moving this space is, we run it month to month so your strategy and budget can adjust as the platform itself changes.
How much should I budget for this?
Because paid placements are still rolling out, early budgets go primarily toward GEO foundations and citation building, with paid spend layered in as placements become available in your market. We’ll walk through current options during onboarding.
