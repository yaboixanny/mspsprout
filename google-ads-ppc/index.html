Google Ads Questions, Answered

The questions we hear most from MSPs before they run their first campaign with us.

Why are managed IT clicks so expensive?
IT support and cybersecurity keywords are some of the most competitive in local services—every MSP in your market is bidding on the same handful of high-intent searches, and the lifetime value of a client is high enough that competitors can afford to pay a lot per click. The fix isn’t avoiding those keywords, it’s tightening targeting, negative keywords, and landing pages so the clicks you do pay for convert at a much higher rate.
How much should an MSP budget for Google Ads?
It depends on your market and how competitive local IT-services keywords are, but most MSPs we work with start seeing meaningful call volume once ad spend is enough to sustain consistent daily impressions in their service area—not just a handful of clicks a day. We’ll size a starting budget with you based on your specific market during onboarding rather than quoting a one-size-fits-all number.
How long before Google Ads starts producing leads?
Unlike SEO, Google Ads can start driving calls and form fills within days of launch since you’re paying for placement instead of waiting to rank organically. That said, the first couple of weeks are typically a tuning period—we’re watching search terms, adjusting bids, and cutting anything that isn’t converting before scaling spend on what works.
Do you require a long-term contract?
No. Google Ads budgets are managed month to month, so you can scale spend up or down—or pause entirely—based on what’s actually converting. We’d rather earn the account every month than lock you into a term.
What’s the difference between Google Ads and SEO for lead generation?
Google Ads buys immediate placement at the top of search results, so it’s the faster path to leads while your organic rankings are still building. SEO takes longer to gain traction but keeps generating traffic without paying per click once you rank. Most of the MSPs we work with eventually run both together—Ads for immediate volume, SEO compounding in the background.
