What Is MSP SEO?

MSP SEO is search engine optimization built specifically for managed service providers. It means ranking your site for the searches IT buyers actually run, like “managed IT services [city],” “IT support company,” or “cybersecurity for [industry],” and turning that traffic into qualified calls.

Generic SEO chases traffic. MSP SEO is part of your wider MSP marketing system and is measured by booked calls from businesses that fit your client profile.

How to Choose an MSP SEO Agency

Here’s how to judge any SEO agency, including us, before you sign.

Red Flags

  • Guaranteed rankings or “page one in 30 days”
  • No experience with MSPs or IT buyers
  • Reports full of traffic and impressions but no calls or leads
  • Long lock-in contracts with no exit
  • Generic, mass-produced content with no input from you
  • Bought links or “secret” tactics they can’t explain

Questions to Ask

  • Have you worked with MSPs, and what happened to their lead flow?
  • How will you measure calls and leads, not just rankings?
  • What happens in the first 90 days?
  • Who writes the content, and do I approve it before it publishes?
  • Who owns the content, site changes, and accounts if we part ways?
  • How will you handle AI search, like ChatGPT and AI Overviews?

MSP SEO FAQs

The questions we hear most from MSPs before starting an SEO program with us.

How long does MSP SEO take to show results?
Most clients see initial ranking movement within 90 days, though competitive local markets can take longer. SEO compounds over time rather than producing instant results like paid ads. See the timeline above for what we work on in months 1 to 3, 4 to 6, and 6 to 12.
How many keywords will you target?
It depends on your market and your plan. We focus on a tight set of buying-intent keywords, each mapped to a specific page, instead of chasing a long list of terms that bring traffic but no calls.
What leads can I expect from SEO?
We don’t promise lead counts, because they depend on your market, your competition, and your site. What we do is track every call and form back to the page and keyword that produced it, so you see real numbers every month instead of guesses.
Should I do SEO myself or hire an agency?
You can start on your own if you have the time to write consistently and the technical skills to fix site issues. Our guide to common MSP SEO mistakes shows what to watch for. Most MSP owners hire out because their time is worth more spent selling and serving clients.
Is SEO dead now that AI exists?
No. AI search tools pull from the same kind of clear, specific, trustworthy content that SEO has always rewarded. The fundamentals still decide who gets found, and they now also decide who gets cited in AI answers.
Can my MSP show up in ChatGPT?
It’s possible, but there’s no shortcut and no guarantee. It comes down to authority and presence: content that answers buyers’ questions and credible sources that vouch for you. Read how to rank your MSP on ChatGPT, or see our AI search optimization (GEO) service.
Do you use AI content, and does Google penalize it?
Google’s guidance focuses on whether content is helpful and trustworthy, not on how it was produced. Whatever tools are involved, every page is built around real buyer questions, and you review and approve everything before it publishes.
Do you work with my existing website?
Yes. Every program starts with an audit of your current site. We fix what’s holding it back first, then build new pages and content on top of it.
How do you report results?
You get a monthly report covering rankings, organic traffic, calls, and leads, plus an optional growth call to walk through what’s working and where to double down next.
Do you require a long-term contract?
We recommend at least a few months to let rankings start moving, but there’s no multi-year lock-in. You can adjust or pause the program as your needs change.
