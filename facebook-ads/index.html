Meta Ads Questions, Answered

The questions we hear most from MSPs before running their first Facebook or Instagram campaign with us.

Do Facebook ads actually work for B2B IT services?
Yes, when they’re targeted correctly. Business owners and IT decision-makers spend time on Facebook and Instagram like everyone else—the key is targeting by business ownership and job title instead of broad interest categories, and pointing ads at a landing page built to convert instead of just driving page likes.
How is this different from just boosting a post?
Boosting a post optimizes for engagement, not leads. We build dedicated campaigns with custom audiences, conversion-tracked landing pages, and retargeting—structured the same way a real lead-generation funnel should be, not a vanity-metrics play.
What budget do I need to get started?
It depends on your market size and how competitive local IT-services audiences are, but we’ll size a starting budget with you during onboarding based on your specific service area rather than quoting a generic number.
Can you run Instagram ads too?
Yes. Every Meta campaign we build runs across both Facebook and Instagram placements by default, and we adjust the mix based on where your audience actually engages.
Do you require a long-term contract?
No. Meta ad budgets are managed month to month, so you can scale spend up or down based on what’s actually converting.
