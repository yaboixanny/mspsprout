Appointment Setting Questions, Answered

The questions we hear most from MSPs before starting an appointment setting program with us.

Who actually books the appointments—a bot or a real person?
A real person. Automated chat and booking tools have their place, but the calls and messages that actually convert into booked appointments come from trained live agents who can handle objections and answer real questions.
How fast do you respond to new leads?
Speed-to-lead is one of the biggest factors in conversion, so leads are contacted within minutes during business hours, not hours or days later.
Do you only book appointments from MSP Sprout campaigns, or from all our lead sources?
We can work leads from any source—your website, referrals, past campaigns, or other channels—not just leads MSP Sprout generates directly.
What happens to leads that don’t book right away?
We run a structured follow-up sequence across call, text, and email instead of giving up after one attempt, since most appointments come from the third or later touch, not the first.
Do you require a long-term contract?
No. Appointment setting is managed month to month so you can scale based on what’s actually converting into booked calls.
