Bing Ads Questions, Answered

The questions we hear most from MSPs who’ve never tried Microsoft Advertising before.

Is it really worth advertising on Bing?
Bing holds a meaningfully smaller share of search volume than Google, but the cost per click is typically 20-30% lower for the same keywords, and almost no competitors bother running campaigns there. For most MSPs it’s a low-risk way to pick up additional qualified leads without cannibalizing Google Ads budget.
Do I need a separate strategy from my Google Ads campaigns?
Not from scratch—we adapt the keyword sets, negative keywords, and landing pages already proven on Google Ads for Microsoft Advertising’s platform and audience, so you’re not starting over.
How much should I budget for Bing Ads?
Because cost per click is typically lower than Google, MSPs often see solid volume on a smaller budget. We’ll size a specific starting budget with you during onboarding based on your market.
Does Bing Ads include LinkedIn targeting?
Since Microsoft owns LinkedIn, Microsoft Advertising offers LinkedIn profile targeting (job title, company, industry) as an option layered on top of your search campaigns—something Google Ads simply can’t offer.
Do you require a long-term contract?
No. Bing ad budgets are managed month to month so you can scale spend up or down based on what’s actually converting.
