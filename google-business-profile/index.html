Google Business Profile Questions, Answered

The questions we hear most from MSPs before starting a GBP optimization program with us.

We already have a Google Business Profile—why does it need work?
Having a profile and having an optimized one are very different things. Most MSP profiles are missing service categories, have stale photos, and get few if any new reviews—all signals Google weighs heavily for map pack ranking. An audit almost always turns up several quick, high-impact fixes.
How long before we see movement in the map pack?
Most clients see initial ranking movement within 30-60 days, though the exact timeline depends on how competitive your local market is and how far behind the starting profile was.
Can you help us get more Google reviews?
Yes—review generation is a core part of the program. We set up automated request sequences that make it easy for happy clients to leave a review, and we manage responses to the ones you get.
Is this the same as SEO?
It’s related but distinct. SEO covers your website’s rankings in organic search results; Google Business Profile optimization is specifically about your map pack and local pack visibility, which is a separate ranking system with its own signals.
Do you require a long-term contract?
No. GBP optimization is managed month to month, though most clients stay on since ongoing posting and review generation are what keep a profile actively ranking.
